Who we are

NorthStar Travel Group was established to make travelling affordable, convenient and unforgettable. We specialise in holidays, events, transport and customised travel experiences throughout South Africa and beyond.

Why we started

Because holidays
shouldn't only be
for some people.

Too many South Africans put off travelling because a holiday feels like something you either pay for in full or don't do at all. We started NorthStar to break that down — a small deposit, a payment plan that matches your pay cycle, and someone on the other end of a WhatsApp who actually knows your booking.

We know the roads, the resorts, the border posts and the shortcuts. We know which lodge is worth the money and which "sea-facing" room faces a parking lot. That knowledge is the product; the booking is just the paperwork.

Mission

To make travel accessible to every South African household by combining honest pricing, flexible payment and genuine local knowledge — so that a holiday becomes something you plan for, not something you dream about.

Vision

To be the travel group that Southern African families trust first — the name that comes up when somebody says "who booked that trip for you?"

Promise

Every quote in writing. Every inclusion listed. Every traveller reachable on one number from the moment they book until the moment they're home.

Our values

Five things we
don't compromise on.

These aren't wall posters. They're the rules we use when something goes wrong at 21:00 on a Sunday.

I

Honesty about price

No hidden extras, no "plus levies" discovered at the airport. If it isn't in the quote, we tell you before you pay.

II

Answer the phone

A travel agent who goes quiet is worse than no travel agent. You get a reply, even when the reply is "still working on it".

III

Respect the budget

We quote what you asked for first, and only then show you what an upgrade would cost. Never the other way around.

IV

Look after the group

Families, stokvels, schools and church groups are the backbone of this business. They get the same care as a private charter.

V

Protect your information

Passport copies, ID numbers and payment details are handled in line with POPIA and never shared beyond the suppliers who need them.
