Visa guide for
the neighbours

Border rules change, and they change without much announcement. Treat this as an orientation, not gospel — always confirm the current requirement with us or the relevant embassy before you travel.

The three documents that decide everything

  • A passport valid for at least six months after your return date, with at least two blank facing pages. This is the number-one reason travellers get turned away.
  • Proof of onward travel — a return ticket or itinerary.
  • Proof of accommodation and funds — your booking confirmation and a card in your own name.

An emergency travel document is not a passport substitute at most borders. If yours expires within a year, renew it before booking.

Zimbabwe

South African passport holders generally do not need a visa in advance for tourism; a visitor's entry is issued at the border or airport. Budget for a small entry or tourism levy, and carry cash in US dollars — card facilities at land borders are unreliable.

  • Peak season at Beitbridge is slow. Cross early in the morning, and expect queues over Easter and in December.
  • Driving your own car? You need the vehicle registration papers, a police clearance letter if the car is financed, a ZA sticker, two triangles, a fire extinguisher and reflective vests. Third-party insurance and road access fees are paid at the border.
  • Victoria Falls visitors who want to cross to the Zambian side should ask about the KAZA UniVisa, which covers both countries.

Botswana

South African passport holders are normally granted a visa-free visitor's permit on arrival, typically for up to 90 days per year. Keep the stamped slip you're given — you'll be asked for it on exit.

  • Botswana takes foot-and-mouth control seriously. You'll drive through disinfectant mats and may not carry raw meat, unpasteurised dairy or certain fresh produce across the border.
  • Road tax and insurance levies are payable for private vehicles at the border, in pula or rand.
  • Fly-in safaris into the Okavango have strict luggage limits — usually 20 kg in a soft bag.

Mozambique

South African passport holders do not require a visa for short tourist visits. Other nationalities usually can obtain a visa on arrival at main entry points, but this should be confirmed in advance rather than assumed.

  • Ponta do Ouro and Kosi Bay: the road in requires a 4x4 in wet conditions. Ask us before booking a sedan.
  • Carry your driving licence, vehicle papers, a permission letter if the car isn't yours, and buy third-party insurance at the border.
  • Two reflective vests, two warning triangles and a "ZA" sticker are compulsory for South African vehicles.
  • Keep small notes in meticais for tolls and market purchases.
Travelling with children? Southern African borders routinely ask for an unabridged birth certificate for every minor, plus an affidavit of consent from any parent not travelling. Carry originals or certified copies — a photograph on a phone is not accepted.

Common reasons people get turned back

  • Passport with less than six months' validity, or no blank pages
  • Missing birth certificate or parental consent affidavit for a minor
  • No proof of return travel or accommodation
  • Vehicle papers in someone else's name without a signed authorisation letter
  • Arriving at a border post after closing time — several land posts close between 18:00 and 22:00
