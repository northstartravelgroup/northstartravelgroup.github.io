Payments
& refunds

A holiday shouldn't need to be paid for in one go. Here's exactly how the money works — deposits, instalments, methods, and what happens if plans change.

Step one

Deposit

From R500 per person to confirm and hold your booking. Deposits are non-refundable but can be moved to another date once, free of charge, if you give us at least 30 days' notice.

Step two

Payment plan

The balance is split into instalments across the months before departure. You choose the dates to suit your pay cycle. No interest, no credit check, no penalty for paying early.

Step three

Final balance

Due 45 days before departure for packages with flights, and 21 days before for local coach and self-drive trips. Tickets and vouchers are issued once the balance clears.

How to pay

Accepted
payment methods.

Always get a written confirmation and a receipt for every payment. If you haven't received one within 24 hours, phone us.

EFT / bank transfer

Preferred. Use your invoice number as the reference and send us the proof of payment.

Cash deposit

Accepted at the bank counter. Bank charges are for the payer's account.

Card payment

By arrangement — we send a secure payment link. Card fees may apply on some transactions.

Instant EFT

Through your banking app, using the reference on your invoice.

Banking details

Banking details are issued on your invoice, not published on this website. This protects you: if anyone sends you bank details in a WhatsApp message or an email that doesn't match your invoice, phone us on 082 500 2003 before paying. We will never ask you to pay into a personal account or change our banking details by email.

If plans change

Cancellation
& refunds.

Airlines, hotels and lodges each have their own rules and they charge us when a booking cancels. The scale below is what we apply on our own packages.

Notice before departureAmount refundedNotes
More than 60 daysEverything paid, less the depositDeposit can be moved to a new date instead
60 – 31 days50% of the totalSupplier penalties may increase this
30 – 15 days25% of the totalFlights are usually non-refundable by this point
Under 15 days or no-showNo refundTravel insurance may cover you — claim through the insurer

Changing names or dates

Name changes and date moves are usually possible for a fee set by the airline or property. Ask us first — never cancel a booking yourself to rebook it.

How refunds are paid

Approved refunds are paid by EFT to the account that made the payment, within 14 working days of the supplier releasing the funds to us.
