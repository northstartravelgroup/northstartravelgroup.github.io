How do I book?

Send us an enquiry — through the form on this site, on WhatsApp, or by phone. Tell us the destination, roughly when you want to travel, how many people, and your budget.

We reply with a written quote within one working day, listing exactly what is included and what isn't. If you're happy, pay the deposit and we confirm the booking with the airline, hotel or lodge and send you a written confirmation.

Can I pay monthly?

Yes, and most of our clients do. A deposit from R500 per person holds the booking, and the balance is split into instalments over the months before you travel. You choose the payment dates to match when you get paid.

There's no interest, no credit check and no penalty for settling early. The only rule is that the full balance must clear before the deadline on your invoice — 45 days before departure for holidays with flights, 21 days for local trips.

Are deposits refundable?

No — deposits are non-refundable, because we use them to secure your seats and rooms with suppliers who charge us immediately.

They are, however, transferable. Give us at least 30 days' notice and we'll move your deposit to a different date or a different package once, free of charge.

Can I cancel?

You can cancel at any time by telling us in writing — WhatsApp or email is fine. What you get back depends on how much notice you give, because airlines and hotels charge us penalties that increase as the date approaches.

The full sliding scale is set out on the payments page. Never cancel a flight or hotel booking directly with the supplier — always come through us, or you may lose the right to a refund entirely.

How do refunds work?

Once a cancellation is approved, we claim the refundable portion back from each supplier. When they release the funds to us, we pay you by EFT into the account the payment came from — normally within 14 working days.

Airline refunds are the slowest part of the process and can take several weeks. We'll keep you updated rather than leaving you wondering.

Do you arrange passports?

We don't issue passports — only the Department of Home Affairs does that. What we do is tell you exactly what you need, help you book the appointment, check your documents before you go, and make sure your passport has enough validity and blank pages for the trip.

Most countries require your passport to be valid for at least six months after your return date, with two blank pages. Check yours early — this is the single most common reason people miss a trip.

Do you arrange visas?

We assist with visas. That means we confirm the requirement for your nationality and destination, give you the document checklist, help complete and review the forms, book the appointment where needed, and provide the itinerary and confirmation letters the embassy asks for.

The decision to grant or refuse a visa belongs to the embassy or consulate alone. We can't guarantee an outcome, and visa fees are never refundable.

What about travelling with children?

Children under 18 travelling across South African borders need an unabridged birth certificate, and a parental consent affidavit if one or both parents aren't travelling with them. We'll tell you exactly which documents apply to your family and check them before you leave.

Most of our resorts and lodges have reduced child rates and family rooms — tell us the ages and we'll quote accordingly.

Is travel insurance included?

Not unless your quote says so. We strongly recommend it on every trip and can arrange it through licensed providers. It's the only thing that protects you if you have to cancel for medical reasons, or need help while you're away.

Do you handle groups?

Yes — stokvels, churches, schools, sports clubs, corporate teams and family reunions. Groups of ten or more qualify for reduced rates, and on coach packages every eleventh traveller goes free.

For groups we set up one payment schedule, one contact person, and a name list we manage for you.
