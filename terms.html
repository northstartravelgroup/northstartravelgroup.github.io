1. Booking terms

These terms apply between NorthStar Travel Group (Pty) Ltd ("NorthStar", "we", "us") and any person who requests, confirms or pays for a booking ("the client", "you"). Paying a deposit means you have read and accepted these terms on behalf of everyone travelling on that booking.

  • Quotes are valid for 7 days and are subject to availability and to exchange-rate and supplier price movement until a deposit is received.
  • Deposits are non-refundable but transferable once, with at least 30 days' notice, to another date or package.
  • Balances are due 45 days before departure for packages including flights, and 21 days before for local coach and self-drive packages. Bookings with unpaid balances after the due date may be cancelled and the deposit forfeited.
  • Prices are quoted per person sharing in South African rand unless stated otherwise, and exclude anything listed under "excluded" on your quote.
  • Cancellations must be sent to us in writing. The refund scale on our payments page applies, subject to any stricter supplier penalty which we will pass on at cost.
  • Amendments to names, dates or routing are subject to supplier fees plus an administration fee, which we will quote before making any change.

2. Travel conditions

  • Documents. It is your responsibility to travel with a valid passport (usually with at least six months' validity beyond your return date and two blank pages), the correct visas, unabridged birth certificates and parental consent affidavits for minors, and any required vaccination certificates. We advise and check, but the responsibility remains yours.
  • Punctuality. Departure times for coaches, shuttles and flights are fixed. Travellers who arrive late may be left behind and no refund is due.
  • Conduct. Suppliers may refuse to carry or accommodate a traveller who is intoxicated, abusive or a danger to others. No refund is due in those circumstances and repatriation is at the traveller's cost.
  • Health and fitness. Some itineraries involve walking, boats, light aircraft, altitude or long road transfers. Tell us in advance about any medical condition, mobility need, allergy or pregnancy so we can advise and arrange properly.
  • Insurance. Comprehensive travel insurance is strongly recommended on every booking and is required on some international itineraries.

4. Privacy policy & POPIA

We process personal information in line with the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, ID or passport number, date of birth, phone number, email and address.
  • Travel details: destinations, dates, dietary and medical requirements you choose to share, and loyalty numbers.
  • Payment records: proof of payment and invoice history. We do not store full card numbers.

Why we collect it

Solely to quote, book, issue tickets and vouchers, meet supplier and border requirements, invoice you, and contact you about your trip. Marketing messages are sent only if you opt in, and every one includes a way to opt out.

Who we share it with

Only the suppliers required to fulfil your booking — airlines, hotels, lodges, transport operators, insurers, and immigration or border authorities where legally required. We never sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

For five years after your last booking, to meet tax and consumer-protection record-keeping requirements, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

You may ask us at any time to confirm what information we hold about you, correct it, delete it where we are not legally required to keep it, or object to direct marketing. Write to singwane2004@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

5. Cookie policy

This website is deliberately light on tracking. It does not set advertising cookies of its own. The following third-party services may set cookies or store data when you use the site:

  • Google Fonts — to load the typefaces used on this site.
  • Google Maps — only on the contact page, when the map loads.
  • Analytics — if enabled, Google Analytics measures anonymous, aggregated page visits so we can see which destinations interest people. It does not identify you personally.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. The site will continue to work normally.
