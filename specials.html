How the deals work

The small print,
in plain language.

01

Offers are subject to availability

Suppliers release a limited number of discounted seats and rooms. When they're gone, the standard rate applies — we'll always tell you before you pay.

02

One offer per booking

Discounts can't be stacked unless we say so in writing. If two offers could apply, we'll apply the better one for you.

03

Deposits still apply

A discounted holiday still needs a deposit to hold it. The discount is applied to the total before the payment plan is worked out.

04

Student and group rates need proof

A valid student card, or a group list with names and ID numbers, at the time of booking.
