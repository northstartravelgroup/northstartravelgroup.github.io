How to pay off a
holiday monthly

The reason most people don't travel isn't that a holiday costs too much. It's that a holiday asks for all its money at once. Break the same amount into four or five months and a trip that felt impossible becomes an ordinary line in the budget.

Start from the date, not the price

Pick when you want to go first, then count backwards. A December beach trip planned in August gives you four pay cheques. The same trip planned in November gives you one — and that's what makes holidays feel expensive.

As a rough guide: divide the total by the number of pay dates between now and 45 days before departure. That's your monthly figure. If it's uncomfortable, move the date out by two months rather than cutting the trip in half.

What the deposit actually does

A deposit — with us, from R500 per person — isn't a fee. It's what locks in today's price and holds the seats and rooms. Airfares and lodge rates climb as availability drops, so booking early at a fixed price is usually cheaper than saving up and booking later.

The trade-off is that a deposit is non-refundable, because the supplier charges us for the hold immediately. Make sure you're serious about the dates before you pay it.

Ask for the payment schedule in writing, with the exact dates and amounts. A good agent will give it to you without being asked.

A worked example

Say a family of four is going to Durban in December at R4 850 per person — R19 400 in total. Booked in August:

  • August: deposit of R2 000 (R500 × 4) — booking confirmed, price locked
  • September: R5 800
  • October: R5 800
  • November: R5 800 — balance settled well before the 21-day deadline

Nobody had to find R19 400 in one month, and nobody paid interest.

Five rules that keep it working

  • Pay on the day you're paid. Set a debit order or a calendar reminder for the same day each month. Money that sits in a current account gets spent.
  • Keep every proof of payment. One folder on your phone. Reconcile it against your invoice each month.
  • Budget the extras separately. Spending money, meals not included, curios, tips and airport parking are real costs. Add 15–20% of the package price and save that too.
  • Take the insurance. It's the only thing that protects the money you've paid if someone gets sick and you have to cancel.
  • Tell your agent early if the plan changes. Notice is worth money. Thirty days' warning usually means we can move your booking; three days usually means we can't.

Groups and stokvels

Group trips work beautifully on this model — but appoint one person to hold the list and one person to talk to the agent. Every member pays their own instalments against their own name on the booking, never into a member's personal account.

The scam to watch for

If you ever receive a message saying banking details have "changed", stop and phone your consultant on the number you already have. Legitimate agencies don't change banking details by email or WhatsApp. This one check has saved travellers a great deal of money.
